# PatchAcceptance

## Inputs

- Consume ReleaseRollForward's OriginalPatchPurposeOwner decision before any transport or source acceptance.
- Read the complete selected Unit or Flat Patch, native owners and affected consumers, retained frontiers, Established policy and ToolchainTarget, and current and applicable source version-root notes.
- Use first-commit contracts and direct prerequisite contracts as source locators, subject to that authority-first decision.
- Historical exports remain read-only purpose evidence and unchanged transport input.
- Authored Rust source consumes the installed rust skill's [construction owner](/codex/skills/rust/references/rust.md) and its `PurposeBoundRustChange`.

## Semantic Acceptance

- The product agent performs cumulative source acceptance synchronously in the linear Roll.
- Before dependent edits, resolve the complete assignment and Rust construction, including effective-configuration coverage for every requested entrypoint.
- Before publication or post-check continuation, compare the cumulative responsibility with its native base through rust's [Semantic review](/codex/skills/rust/references/rust/post-green-review.md).
- Include temporary Main corrections and all Child Patches, not only the latest hunk or selected tests.
- For each approved effect, locate the actual producer, consumer, lifecycle, failure and cleanup implementation.
- Check that native-supplied behavior has not been claimed as a private dependency or duplicated by the candidate.
- For performance-motivated additions, consume ReleaseRollForward's measured-benefit decision against the actual consumer and reject narrower-workload, incomplete-result or unmeasured claims.
- Reject missing effects, incoming duties and new or relocated Patches outside the separately authorized structural assignment, independently of compiler or test outcomes.
- Compare an approved change with the original obligation, requested difference and complete replacement under ReleaseRollForward's disablement-versus-retirement decision.
- For local disablement, inspect the complete activation path and cumulative diff to prove the requested absence while preserving continuing upstream implementation and its tests.
- For authorized retirement, prove exclusive source and consumers are removed while shared and independently required behavior remain intact.
- Missing, overlapping or contradictory ownership returns to the purpose decision before further edits.
- A Unit that cannot be reasoned about coherently returns to responsibility design; splitting compensating code into Child Patches does not establish a sound boundary.
- Check Purpose, Requires, Provides, Boundary, Lane, CrossGroup and Segment against the supported decision and actual implementation, not against each other as proof.
- Review the action, object and method-or-scope parts of every selected Unit, Flat Patch and Child Patch name against PatchFormat and actual behavior.
- At group closure or a stop before deferred consumers, review the whole approved functional relationship and its exclusions without promoting deferred features.
- Cross acceptance requires the complete `relationships.txt` file from the current working lane and review under PatchExport's diagram contract.
- Reject any other-lane content, including draft, even when a dependency or future integration is real.
- Verify that one compact graph makes current cross functions and their relationships clear without reconstructing repeated fragments.
- Missing, stale or unsupported diagram relationships return to OriginalPatchPurposeOwner before continuation or export; a present file alone proves no semantic acceptance.
- Any counterexample returns to OriginalPatchPurposeOwner's complete dependent-decision rejection before further transport or publication.
- Explicit withdrawal authority is required for every omitted effect; a test-only, no-op or truncated replacement cannot close it.
- Mechanical line limits and overrides do not change this acceptance boundary.

## User-Selected Batch Review

- Read the indexed task's SubagentReview setting before selecting subagents for a requested review.
- A yes value permits one fresh read-only leaf subagent to review a selected group of at least two distinct Units or Flat Patches.
- A no value keeps the review in the product agent.
- Multiple Child Patches in one Unit do not satisfy the group requirement.
- Keep source authoring, testing, Git effects, publication, installation and final acceptance in the product agent.
- Supply the reviewer's exact scope, requirements, complete relevant source and history, applicable notes, verification evidence and excluded locators through the native task message.
- Return findings to the existing product owner and its ordinary Unit or Flat Patch correction boundary.
- Retain the native reviewer handle until completion and drain it before any successor that depends on its result.
- The setting selects no automatic per-Unit dispatch, additional review gate, review queue or task progress state.

## Responsibility Closure

- Close the complete assigned behavior and its product terminal before reducing pending work.
- Required construction, fixture and direct-entrypoint seams belong to their producer before formal or draft dependents are authored.
- Preserve the draft-independent formal prefix and every admitted consumer.
- Missing evidence, borrowed behavior, cross-Patch compensation or rejected construction keeps the responsibility open.
- Publish the first-commit contract once and retain each Child Patch's distinct source responsibility.
- Commit or terminal Revise formats and publishes every final Patch within PatchFormat's canonical layout.
- A partial ChildPatchSequence remains open with its unfinished obligations.
- Report semantic acceptance, native structural admission and selected test outcomes separately.
- Native transport proves structure, bytes, formatting and rollback only.
- Closure and successful checks do not authorize changed scope or contracts; consume ReleaseRollForward's scope decision before continuation.
- Focused tests answer source questions after publication; automatic reconstruction and non-idempotent fixup folds require their corresponding focused tests before advancement.
- Prior failures remain evidence without creating a full-suite gate.
- Git frontier coverage and semantic closure retain their own evidence requirements.
- Lane closure consumes TaskWorkspace's complete admitted-inventory comparison rather than merely the subset that reached Apply.
- An audit requested for the whole patch set includes every admitted responsibility, child, omission and approved replacement, and separates content, naming and verification findings.

## Test And Generated-Output Boundaries

- Consume Rust's actual-entrypoint and shared-fixture proof before correcting a premise at its assigned owner, preserving assertions for all affected approved effects.
- Focused snapshots remain private Runner evidence and never enter a Flat Patch, Child Patch, or compatibility artifact.
- Apply, Commit, and Revise transport source only.
- Manual reconstruction never copies generated postimages.
- Consume a committed formatted Apply or ExecutableTransform tip without a duplicate commit.
- Terminal-chrome package diagnostics and automatic capture retain renderer and continuation-cell evidence privately.
- Selected Finalize owns authoritative generated tail rather than ordinary Patch closure.
- A dependency Unit consumes RepositoryMaintenance's fixed Cargo and separate Bazel recipes and publishes both lock postimages with its admitted dependency inputs.
- Other source edits stay outside maintenance admission.
- Configuration-type or schema-description changes consume fixed config-schema generation on the clean source tip, review its fixture, and fold a changed postimage through same-Unit Revise.
- Repository generation owns JSON bytes and selected schema comparison validates them.
- No schema or snapshot Child Patch, producer ledger, or extra verification route is created.

## Platform Scope

```
EstablishedTargetAcceptanceOwner
+-- Consumer: PlatformScopedGateConsumer
+-- Input: selected Unit or Flat Patch responsibility complete source callers and semantic history
+-- Before: every rewrite's first dependent source edit
+-- Gate: Established ToolchainTarget executes
+-- Scope: current user decisions and version-root notes
+-- Boundary: approved platform criterion at each owned effect and consumer
+-- Resolve: retain remove or simplify every owned condition against that criterion
+-- Admission: all owned conditions resolved before authoring
+-- Exclude: other-target-exclusive tests and snapshots
+-- Proof: complete source and history preserve required in-scope behavior
+-- Finding: out-of-scope selects no edit
+-- Breach: unsupported platform conditions return to ReleaseRollForward
+-- Successor: SharedSourceContainmentSuccessor
`-- Terminal: ScopedProductAcceptanceTerminal
```

- Product platform scope and compatibility policy come from current user decisions and version notes, not a skill default or the execution host. A target label alone justifies neither blanket gating nor removal of unrelated upstream boundaries.
- Before any dependent edit, including resumed rewrites, read complete retained and current source, callers, tests, dependencies, and introduction history. Resolve every owned platform condition against the approved criterion; later audits or passing tests cannot replace this decision. Reopen it when source, scope, or design changes.
- Inspect cfg, cfg_attr, cfg!, runtime checks, imports, modules, build scripts, target dependencies, wrappers, tests, and indirect guards, including unchanged conditions governing the selected behavior. Read both branches and consumers; search matches are not proof.
- Compare each owned branch with shared or unconditional alternatives before retaining, removing, or simplifying it. Preserve required behavior and assertions under the approved compatibility policy; leave unrelated upstream code untouched. Unresolved conditions block dependent authoring, publication, and closure through the existing ReleaseRollForward decision and repair boundary.
- Audit complete normalization before declaring the lane closed. Derive Patch order from Git, inspect cumulative changes and affected source, and resolve findings in the assigned Unit or Flat Patch.

## Closure And Publication

- Complete direct semantic review and assigned behavior before TaskWorkspace reduction and the next selection.
- A discovered material defect returns to its actual Unit or Flat Patch through the existing Current revision or earlier-Unit Fork route.
- Private-combination defects remain in their corresponding original Units; discovery timing cannot justify a new integration owner.
- A demonstrated standalone upstream defect consumes ReleaseRollForward's shared bugfix source change, including affected baseline measurement and passing regression evidence before comparison reuse.
- Preserve actual later source, conflicts, unfinished behavior, retained parents, and declared suffix rather than restoring saved task text or relabeling work.
- If a defect cannot be consumed yet, retain only its unresolved cause and successor in the existing task.
- Do not create a review task, finding registry, placeholder commit, stash, or alternate recovery topology.
- At closure and reentry consume GitCustody's retained-frontier coverage and native reader cleanup before the next Patch or Fork.
- Before lane or final publication, compare cumulative behavior and structural costs of every Unit and Flat Patch with current authority and approved redistributions.
- Reuse unchanged local acceptance evidence and inspect unresolved cross-contract interactions rather than repeating complete unrelated source reads.
- Clean Apply and unchanged order do not prove correct responsibility placement.
- Export proves its closed-prefix publication only.
- Complete-suite Finalize occurs only when user-selected and reports its actual outcome without granting mutation or release-completion authority.
