# PatchAcceptance

## Inputs

- Read the selected Unit or Flat Patch, native owners and consumers, retained frontiers, Established policy and ToolchainTarget, and applicable working and source notes: version root first, then lane.
- Consume ReleaseRollForward's complete selected responsibility and reconstruction boundary before accepting a corrective design or test premise.
- Historical exports remain read-only purpose evidence and unchanged transport input.
- Authored Rust source consumes the installed lang skill's [Rust language owner](/codex/skills/lang/references/rust.md) and its `PurposeBoundRustChange`.

## Linear Source Acceptance

- The product agent owns semantic acceptance synchronously in the same linear Roll.
- There is no subagent gate, dispatch handle, asynchronous finding, review queue, or review-only drain.
- Before dependent edits, consume ReleaseRollForward's scope decision and Rust's complete construction.
- Before source publication or post-check continuation, review the cumulative owning Unit or Flat Patch, including temporary Main changes, against its native base through lang's [Rust Semantic review](/codex/skills/lang/references/rust/post-green-review.md).
- Compare every assigned effect with actual complete production and consumer seams, including native-supplied behavior, rather than the selected tests alone.
- Then compare all candidate additions with that assignment, rejecting unapproved incoming duties. Rust owns structural-cost and fixture-proof decisions.
- Every omitted effect requires existing explicit withdrawal authority; implementation rejection, shorter source and green tests grant none.
- A test-only, no-op or truncated replacement fails this comparison even if native transport accepts its metadata and its focused checks pass.
- The 1500-line finding is a mechanical publication ceiling, not a quality threshold or available authoring budget.
- A final correct hunk, split Child Patches or line-limit override cannot justify a cumulatively rejected candidate.
- A new or rewritten Patch also consumes the existing effective-configuration coverage decision for every requested entrypoint.

## Responsibility Closure

- Each Unit or Flat Patch closes its assigned seam, effect, consumer, cleanup, and product terminal.
- Schema remains maximal before its generated transform.
- Producers close construction, fixture, and direct entrypoint seams required by approved dependents before dependent authoring.
- Missing facts, omitted obligations, borrowed behavior, compensation by another Lane child or a rejected construction block closure.
- Preserve approved assignments and reject unapproved incoming behavior independently of compiler and test outcomes.
- Each final Flat Patch or Child Patch is formatted and published by Commit or terminal Revise.
- Partial ChildPatchSequence keeps its Unit open and preserves its unfinished obligations.
- Native transport proves its declared structure, bytes, formatting, and rollback, not semantic quality.
- Selected focused tests answer actual source questions after publication.
- Automatic reconstruction and non-idempotent fixup folds consume their corresponding focused tests before advancement.
- A prior failure remains evidence rather than a universal full-suite or next-Patch gate.
- Semantic closure and Git frontier coverage remain independent of a passing check.

## Test And Generated-Output Boundaries

- Consume Rust's actual-entrypoint and shared-fixture proof before correcting a premise at its assigned owner, preserving assertions for all affected approved effects.
- Focused snapshots remain private Runner evidence and never enter a Flat Patch, Child Patch, or compatibility artifact.
- Apply, Commit, and Revise transport source only.
- Manual reconstruction never copies generated postimages.
- Consume a committed formatted Apply or ExecutableTransform tip without a duplicate commit.
- Terminal-chrome package diagnostics and automatic capture retain renderer and continuation-cell evidence privately.
- Selected Finalize owns authoritative generated tail rather than ordinary Patch closure.
- A dependency Unit consumes RepositoryMaintenance's fixed Cargo and separate Bazel recipes and publishes both lock postimages with its admitted dependency inputs.
- Other source edits stay outside maintenance admission.
- Configuration-type or schema-description changes consume fixed config-schema generation on the clean source tip, review its fixture, and fold a changed postimage through same-Unit Revise.
- Repository generation owns JSON bytes and selected schema comparison validates them.
- No schema or snapshot Child Patch, producer ledger, or extra verification route is created.

## Platform Scope

```
EstablishedTargetAcceptanceOwner
+-- Consumer: PlatformScopedGateConsumer
+-- Input: selected Unit or Flat Patch responsibility complete source callers and semantic history
+-- Before: every rewrite's first dependent source edit
+-- Gate: Established ToolchainTarget executes
+-- Scope: current user decisions and version-root notes
+-- Boundary: approved platform criterion at each owned effect and consumer
+-- Resolve: retain remove or simplify every owned condition against that criterion
+-- Admission: all owned conditions resolved before authoring
+-- Exclude: other-target-exclusive tests and snapshots
+-- Proof: complete source and history preserve required in-scope behavior
+-- Finding: out-of-scope selects no edit
+-- Breach: unsupported platform conditions return to ReleaseRollForward
+-- Successor: SharedSourceContainmentSuccessor
`-- Terminal: ScopedProductAcceptanceTerminal
```

- Product platform scope and compatibility policy come from current user decisions and version notes, not a skill default or the execution host. A target label alone justifies neither blanket gating nor removal of unrelated upstream boundaries.
- Before any dependent edit, including resumed rewrites, read complete retained and current source, callers, tests, dependencies, and introduction history. Resolve every owned platform condition against the approved criterion; later audits or passing tests cannot replace this decision. Reopen it when source, scope, or design changes.
- Inspect cfg, cfg_attr, cfg!, runtime checks, imports, modules, build scripts, target dependencies, wrappers, tests, and indirect guards, including unchanged conditions governing the selected behavior. Read both branches and consumers; search matches are not proof.
- Compare each owned branch with shared or unconditional alternatives before retaining, removing, or simplifying it. Preserve required behavior and assertions under the approved compatibility policy; leave unrelated upstream code untouched. Unresolved conditions block dependent authoring, publication, and closure through the existing ReleaseRollForward decision and repair boundary.
- Audit complete normalization before declaring the lane closed. Derive Patch order from Git, inspect cumulative changes and affected source, and resolve findings in the assigned Unit or Flat Patch.

## Closure And Publication

- Complete direct semantic review and assigned behavior before TaskWorkspace reduction and the next selection.
- A discovered material defect returns to its actual Unit or Flat Patch through the existing Current revision or earlier-Unit Fork route.
- Preserve actual later source, conflicts, unfinished behavior, retained parents, and declared suffix rather than restoring saved task text or relabeling work.
- If a defect cannot be consumed yet, retain only its unresolved cause and successor in the existing task.
- Do not create a review task, finding registry, placeholder commit, stash, or alternate recovery topology.
- At closure and reentry consume GitCustody's retained-frontier coverage and native reader cleanup before the next Patch or Fork.
- Before lane or final publication, compare cumulative behavior and structural costs of every Unit and Flat Patch with current authority and approved redistributions.
- Clean Apply and unchanged order do not prove correct responsibility placement.
- Export proves its closed-prefix publication only.
- Complete-suite Finalize occurs only when user-selected and reports its actual outcome without granting mutation or release-completion authority.
